# Cycle 39 research application — production-authoritative taste loop

Cycle 39 did not reopen aesthetic thresholds or add an uncited taste heuristic. It
translated the frozen Cycle 37 synthesis and Cycle 38 schemas into the production
execution path. The governing sources remain the canonical
[research synthesis](taste-certification/RESEARCH.md) and
[protocol](taste-certification/PROTOCOL.md): real decoded browser evidence rather
than headers, pointwise observation before hidden mirrored pairwise choice, hard
task/accessibility/state vetoes, held-out human-label calibration, uncertainty-aware
statistics, bounded repairs, and explicit machine-versus-human claim semantics.

The implementation decisions preserve those authorities at their boundaries. Capture,
stimulus, calibration, ballot, and statistics records are content-addressed receipts;
three same-base candidates must pass deterministic gates before a blind round robin;
only a unique Condorcet winner becomes `SELECTED_NOT_CERTIFIED`; two repair reservations
are durable and cannot be reset by restart; untrusted taste memory is bounded advice;
and unavailable browser, decoder, accessibility, or panel evidence remains `UNKNOWN`.
Builders never grade themselves, machine configurations never become deciding humans,
and fixture success cannot mint a production certificate.

The resulting engineering chain was verified, but the research claim deliberately
remained open. Cycle 39 acquired no primary corpus, ran no held-out judge calibration,
rendered no frozen old-versus-new brief, collected no deciding human ballot, and made no
claim of global aesthetic superiority. Those unresolved evidence obligations route to
Cycle 40's pinned corpus and live-adapter work, followed by the preregistered twenty-brief
study. TASTE may be a separately labelled secondary audit; it cannot silently replace
the Miniukovich–Figl primary corpus or convert machine preference into human certification.

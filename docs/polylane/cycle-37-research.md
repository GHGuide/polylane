# Cycle 37 research — falsifiable visual taste

Run: `c37-taste-research-20260811-a1`
Scope: `m32.1` research synthesis only.

## Answer

The smallest truthful protocol is a closed, provider-neutral evidence chain:
frozen brief and transformed reference record; three structurally divergent
directions; two browser-rendered candidates; hard task/accessibility/state
gates; sealed pointwise observations; isolated mirrored A/B/B/A human ballot
groups; held-out human-label calibration; cross-brief review; bounded
champion–challenger repairs; and a digest-closed certificate. It is falsifiable
because any missing real render, human receipt, calibration/disjointness proof,
or uncertainty condition prevents a promotion.

The research source registry inspected during integration had 50 source records
and 49 evidence records. The final synthesis uses primary HCI and measurement
research, standards, human-rated dataset contracts, UI-generation benchmark
limitations, and product-design practice. It keeps their authorities separate:
a benchmark can motivate an adapter, a dataset can support calibration, and a
human panel is required for the human-certified claim.

## Decisions frozen for later implementation

- At least 10 varied locked briefs; 5 complete eligible isolated mirrored human
  groups per brief; at least 7 brief wins; pooled human preference >=70%; two-sided
  95% Wilson lower bound >0.50.
- Real browser-rendered desktop 1440x900, mobile 390x844, and every brief-required
  state evidence; decoded-pixel/trace provenance and nonduplicate requirements.
- Pointwise before pairwise; an A/B and B/A exposure to different isolated judges;
  contradiction/one-sided tie/identity leakage/exclusion invalidates a group.
- Held-out human-labelled UI calibration; frozen configuration, 17/24 correct,
  Wilson LCB >=0.50, side and mirror probe exclusions. Machine results never add
  to the human deciding denominator.
- Zero accessibility regression and complete task/state proof are hard vetoes.
- Three direction cards, 3–5 category references plus wildcard, a product-signature
  counterfactual, and cross-brief sameness review prevent both cloning and template
  convergence without treating common motifs as proof of AI authorship or copying.
- At most two evidence-targeted repairs; preserve the incumbent until a challenger
  passes its complete new evidence chain; plateau, rejection, oscillation, third
  repair, or goal drift replan.

## What remains unproven

No data binary was acquired, no browser capture or benchmark was run, no human
label or panel was fabricated, no model was calibrated, and no interface was
certified. Panel identity/independence, assistive-technology experience, IP
determinations, browser/adapter trust, and population representativeness remain
external evidence obligations.

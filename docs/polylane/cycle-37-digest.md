# Cycle 37 digest — research protocol GO

`m32.1` is GO for its research target: eight independent lanes were synthesized
into a falsifiable, provider-neutral visual-taste protocol and threat model.
The protocol requires actual browser renders, non-compensatory task/accessibility
gates, held-out human-label calibration, isolated mirrored human ballots,
uncertainty, product-specificity/cross-brief checks, a two-repair cap, and
truthful claim labels.

This is not a GO for `m32.2`–`m32.5`, `c84`–`c90`, a browser benchmark, a human
panel, or a `TASTE-CERTIFIED` UI. The current visual scripts remain scaffolding;
their red-team false passes are deliberately carried into the future negative
acceptance suite. The next implementation must begin with fail-closed contracts
and fixtures, then acquire external evidence under its own run.

# Cycle 12 digest — visual intelligence and safe skill acquisition

Cycle 12 shipped a reference-grounded visual pipeline: UI work now derives three design
directions from 3–5 relevant products plus one wildcard, freezes a multi-source design lock,
and rejects single-source copying. Missing UI skills can enter only through inert quarantine,
deterministic audit, an isolated with/without benchmark, immutable pinning, project-scoped
installation, and rollback metadata. Promotion requires real desktop/mobile/state image
evidence, three independent visual lenses, at most two repairs, and a ten-product champion
threshold. The first terminal attempt correctly produced NO-GO on the frozen graph budget;
the follow-up repair made 10,000-event validation linear, brought it to 5 seconds, and passed
1,638 tests, ShellCheck, parity, fresh installs, and live GO/NO-GO rehearsal. The real rendered
old-versus-new ten-product corpus remains external and was not converted to prose evidence.

- `bin/polylane-visual-quality.sh` validates image evidence, three verdicts, and repair bounds.

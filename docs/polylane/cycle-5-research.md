# Cycle 5 research — efficiency evidence

The cycle completed in about 12.5 minutes with no recovery launches, which is a large wall-time
improvement over the earlier hour-scale recovery cycle. Its 4.3 million builder tokens are still
disproportionate to the implementation surface, even though much was cached input. The effective
controls were narrow ownership, no delegation, no worker full-suite runs, and selected-skill kits.
The remaining weak point is grading: “zero intervention” and “one host gate” were operational
observations, not yet a machine-issued certificate.

The promoted telemetry primitives make a strict certificate possible. A canary manifest can
freeze expected launches, maximum restarts, maximum wall time, and exactly one terminal gate. The
runner can write a candidate from durable telemetry before acceptance and finalize it only after
cleanup becomes complete. Known token counts must be numeric; unavailable counts remain unknown.
This closes the self-grading gap without adding model work.

# Cycle 37 council — integration decisions

Run: `c37-taste-research-20260811-a1`

| Question | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Is a visual score enough? | No; score observations are diagnostic only. | HCI research separates appearance, perceived usability, task use, credibility, and accessibility. |
| Are models deciding judges? | No. | They can be machine-evaluated or human-calibrated machines, never substitute for deciding human ballots. |
| How can sides be mirrored without memory contamination? | One A/B and one B/A exposure go to two isolated eligible judges in one mirrored group. | It detects order effects while preserving isolation. |
| Are 3–5 references a style transfer recipe? | No; record abstract patterns and transformations only, plus one wildcard. | It retains category fit while banning assets, copy, marks, and distinctive layouts. |
| Does a sameness detector prove low quality/copying/AI authorship? | No; it creates a human review trigger on a separate axis. | Common UI motifs create predictable false positives. |
| Which feedback loop wins? | Champion–challenger with best eligible incumbent preservation and at most two repairs. | Rendered feedback evidence supports bounded repair, while repeated optimization can regress or forget goals. |
| Does a genericness signal block immediately? | No; unresolved review is `UNKNOWN`, known disallowed source reuse is `NOT-CERTIFIED`. | Genericness, quality risk, context fit, and provenance integrity have distinct truth conditions. |

## Dissent and resolved tension

The visual-feedback lane described two initial concepts; anti-homogenization
required three direction cards. The protocol keeps both: exactly three
structural direction cards make divergence inspectable, then exactly two
candidate directions are built and compared while the excluded third direction
is recorded. The judge lane's individual mirrored exposure language was refined
to a two-judge mirrored group so that side reversal does not give the same judge
memory of a live pair.

No council member or integrator has observed a live UI result. These decisions
freeze a research contract, not an aesthetic verdict.
